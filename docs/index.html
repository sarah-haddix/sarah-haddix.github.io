About Me

I am a student at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. I'm currently earning a B.S. in Computer Science and a B.S. in mathematics. I work as an undergraduate reserach assistant in the Real-Time Systems Lab under dept. chair James H. Anderson, where I have mainly worked on projects concerning real-time principals in robotics and AI. Outside of school, I enjoy snowboarding (I am a member of the UNC ski/snowboard team), going to the gym, playing video games (mostly Destiny 2), and reading (mostly Dune).
